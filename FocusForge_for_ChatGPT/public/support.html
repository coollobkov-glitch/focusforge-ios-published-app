Common issues

  • Password reset: use Forgot Password on the login screen and open the newest email link.
  • Restore a purchase: use Restore purchases on the plan screen. Purchases are connected to your Apple ID.
  • Screen Time: allow Family Controls/Screen Time permission when asked so FocusForge can shield selected apps.
  • Delete your account: open Settings and use Delete Account. This removes your FocusForge account and associated app data.
